Publishing Markdown to a LinkedIn Article from an Agent Skill

LinkedIn's article editor saves two heading levels and no inline code, and its page cannot parse HTML. A step by step walk-through of the LinkedIn article destination in publishing-kit, tested by publishing the kit's own walk-through through it.

What is in it:

- 🟢 Multi-line code kept, 3 blocks with the same line counts.
- 🟢 All 4 link targets kept, with the two links around inline code unwrapped.
- 🟢 The table image re-hosted by LinkedIn, alt text restored.
- 🟢 7853 non-space characters in the source and in the saved article.
